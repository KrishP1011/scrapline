## Title
Scrapline

## Short description (one line)
Move. Everything else fights for you — a salvage-yard arena survivor.

## Description
You are a walking scrap rig in a yard that never stops sending things at you. Your
turret fires on its own, so the only thing you control is where you stand.

Survive twenty-second waves, haul the scrap the wrecks drop, and spend it at the
bench between rounds. Every run is built from what the bench offers: forty-two
upgrade cards, ten weapons, and eight automatic abilities that fight for you.
Stack fire and everything burns down around you. Stack crits and your bolts start
arcing between targets. Bolt on a second mount and run two weapons at once.

Something heavy arrives every fifth wave — mini-bosses with one pattern to learn,
and eight main bosses with three phases each, telegraphed attacks and armour that
blunts everything you throw at them. The Smelter bleeds molten slag across the
deck until the arena is a corridor. The Scrapherd hides behind a pen of hostiles
and only becomes vulnerable once you have thinned them out. The Gantry closes and
never stops closing.

Six rigs, each a different run: a glass cannon that folds if you are touched, a
scavenger that hauls more scrap than it fights, and two with a welded mount that
can never be swapped — Ember, whose flame barely scratches anything but sets it
all alight, and Anchor, whose siege cannon spins up to double damage while you
stand perfectly still and collapses the moment you move.

Salvage tokens carry between runs and unlock rigs and permanent workshop upgrades,
then seven prestige ranks after that. There is a fresh daily seed every day that
everyone plays, and twelve badges to go and earn.

No install, no account, and nothing to download — the entire game is one file, and
every sprite, sound effect and music track is generated as it runs.

## Controls
- Move: WASD / arrow keys, or drag anywhere on the left half on touch
- Dash: SPACE or SHIFT, or the on-screen button
- Scrap Blast: E, or the on-screen button
- Buy at the bench: 1-4, R to reroll, ENTER for the next wave
- Pause: ESC or P  ·  Mute: M

Your turret aims and fires by itself. You never aim.
